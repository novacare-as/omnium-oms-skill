## Creating a Support Request

Follow these steps to submit a request that helps our team resolve your issue quickly.

### Write a Clear Subject Line

Summarize the issue in a concise subject. Include the affected feature or area if possible.

> **Good:** _"Payments with AwesomePayment failing on capture"_ **Bad:** _"Hi"_

### Identify Your Environment

Include your **tenant name** (or company name) and the **environment** where the issue occurs (e.g., _Acme Inc, Test environment_).

### Describe the Issue in Detail

Explain what happened, what you expected to happen, and provide a **step-by-step guide** to reproduce the problem. Include any relevant IDs (order IDs, customer IDs, etc.).

### Attach Supporting Evidence

Add **full-context screenshots**, relevant URLs, cURL commands, or API request/response bodies. The more context you provide, the faster we can help.

* * *

## Request Guidelines

One Ticket, One Issue

Submit separate requests for each issue so they can be tracked independently.

Don't Reopen Closed Tickets

Open a new request for ongoing or related issues instead of replying to closed tickets.

Need a Deeper Dive?

For complex issues, you may request a meeting with our support staff for hands-on assistance.

**Do not** add support addresses from other organizations to the request. Support systems from different providers do not interact well with each other.

## Required Information

| # | Information | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Clear subject line | Helps us prioritize and route your issue |
| 2 | Tenant / company name & environment | Lets us locate your data immediately |
| 3 | Detailed issue description | Reduces back-and-forth questions |
| 4 | Steps to reproduce | Allows us to verify and diagnose the problem |
| 5 | Relevant IDs (order, customer, etc.) | Pinpoints the exact records involved |

## Recommended Information

| # | Information | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6 | Full-context screenshots | Visuals often reveal issues text can't |
| 7 | URL (if UI-related) | Takes us directly to the failing page |
| 8 | cURL / API request (if API-related) | Lets us reproduce API issues exactly |
| 9 | Any other relevant details | Context we might not think to ask for |
