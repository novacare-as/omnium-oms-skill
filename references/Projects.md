# Projects

Source: https://docs.omnium.no/docs/Projects

> Manage long-running activities like returns, claims, and complex sales processes using Omnium's project framework.

A project in Omnium handles activities that span a period of time and go beyond a single order. Projects connect collections of carts, orders, and returns with all other data needed for end-to-end process management.

**Common use cases:**

*   Return and claim workflows
*   Long-running sales processes (e.g., kitchen design and installation)
*   Service projects spanning multiple orders
*   Lead collection and tracking
