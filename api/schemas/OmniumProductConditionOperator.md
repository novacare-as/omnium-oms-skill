# OmniumProductConditionOperator

How a product condition compares its attribute. The negative operators also hold for a product without the
attribute.

**Enum** (string): `Equals`, `NotEquals`, `In`, `NotIn`, `GreaterThan`, `GreaterThanOrEqual`, `LessThan`, `LessThanOrEqual`, `Exists`, `NotExists`, `StartsWith`, `EndsWith`
